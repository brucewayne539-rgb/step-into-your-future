## What the portrait does

The student selects a current photo, career, approximate future age, path, grade, and priority. The app removes embedded image metadata, sends a cleaned copy and constrained prompt to OpenAI, receives an illustrative image, adds a permanent “AI-generated / not a prediction” label, and displays the result. The portrait is imaginative, may be inaccurate or biased, and does not predict appearance, ability, career, admission, course placement, licensing, employment, or income.

## Data and retention notice

- The app does not ask for the student’s name, email, student ID, grades, disability information, home address, or school login.
- Application code does not intentionally write the source or result to a student/photo database.
- The hosted portrait feature is to remain disabled unless the district has approved the use and the operator has documented OpenAI Zero Data Retention for the relevant project.
- Provider safety/legal exceptions, server/browser memory, network metadata, device downloads, prints, screenshots, and backups mean the activity must not be described as absolute zero retention or guaranteed secure deletion.
- The source-photo input is cleared after the request; the displayed result is cleared when the page is left. A user/school controls any downloaded or printed copy.

## Rules

- Portrait mode is not available to a person under age 13 in this build.
- The signer must have authority to submit the pictured person’s photo.
- Use occurs only in the approved supervised setting and only for career exploration.
- No public posting, face matching, discipline, grading, admissions, employment, evaluation, targeted advertising, sale, or unrelated reuse is authorized.
- The student may stop before submission and use the no-photo route instead.
