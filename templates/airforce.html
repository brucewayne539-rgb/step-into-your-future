    01 / WHEN YOU JOIN

    UP TO$40,000

    A head start in selected careers

    Entry bonuses are available to qualified active-duty recruits in selected specialties.

    The commitment: A six-year enlistment and successful completion of training. Amounts and payment schedules vary by specialty.

    Check entry bonuses ↗

    02 / KEEP LEARNING

    AT FULL GI BILL ELIGIBILITY100%

    Public in-state tuition & mandatory fees

    The Post-9/11 GI Bill can help qualifying veterans pay for college or approved training. Housing and book support may also be available.

    The qualification: Full coverage requires the 100% benefit level and an eligible public-school program. Private-school limits and other program rules differ.

    Explore GI Bill benefits ↗

    03 / LATER IN YOUR CAREER

    FY2026 AVIATION MAXIMUM$600,000

    An incentive to keep flying

    Certain experienced aviators could qualify for up to $50,000 a year for a 12-year additional service commitment.

    A retention example: This is a total over 12 years, not an entry bonus. Specialty and experience determine eligibility and amount.

    FY2026 application window closed August 31, 2026. Future offers may change.
