    Armie is an independent Step Into Your Future career-exploration demonstration. It connects a fictional student's interests and selected Army career family with courses and opportunities at the student's own high school, then illustrates possible training, education and long-term career pathways.

    No Army career, assignment, training opportunity, rank, education benefit or career outcome is promised. Demo portraits are fictional AI-generated people and are used only to demonstrate the experience.
