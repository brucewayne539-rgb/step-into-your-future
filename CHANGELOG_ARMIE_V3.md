# Armie V3 — Expanded Career Explorer

- Replaced the short Army career list with the six current broad Army career areas used on the official U.S. Army careers site.
- Added 262 specific Army career opportunities, organized behind two clean student-facing dropdowns: Career Area → Specific Army Career.
- Preserved the simple Armie flow: school, grade, career area, specific career, future age, and student interest.
- Simplified service-path handling in the demo so students are not asked to decide enlisted/officer/warrant status before exploring a career; Armie instead tells students to verify the correct path for the selected role.
- Added a reusable career-to-school matching layer that maps each Army role to a related BHS/GHS skill pathway instead of manually writing 262 separate school roadmaps.
- Preserved the V2 future-self journey, GI Bill education panel, privacy link, independent-demo disclaimer, and BHS/GHS-specific course guidance.
- Career names/categories were checked against the U.S. Army Jobs Site Map on September 18, 2026. Availability and requirements can change and must be verified with official Army sources.
