Privacy Policy — current preview

Photos selected on your device

The photo chooser creates temporary previews inside your browser. This preview does not upload those selected files to Picturepurdle, publish them in the mosaic, or send them to an AI image service. Refreshing or closing the page discards the app’s local selection; it does not delete the original files from your device.

The public mosaic

The displayed mosaic uses repeating fictional sample photos and two real founding photos supplied by the operator. Its figures and accelerated portrait sequence illustrate the concept. Selecting a local file does not add that file to the public picture.

Website analytics and cookies

The main preview uses Google Analytics to understand visits and use of the site. Depending on your browser and settings, Google may use cookies and process browser/device details, page activity, and approximate location derived from your internet connection. Our site configuration disables Google Signals and advertising personalization. We do not intentionally send selected photo files or the photo finder’s IDs to Analytics.

Learn more about Google’s use of information from websites and the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Browser privacy controls may also restrict analytics. Private browsing alone does not necessarily prevent measurement.

Hosting, links, and sharing

Render hosts the site and receives the technical information needed to deliver its pages, including network requests and IP addresses. See Render’s privacy information. When you choose a sharing feature or open an external link, your selected app or the destination website handles information under its own policies. Copy-link buttons use the clipboard when you request them.

Messages to us

If you email us, we receive your address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer and manage your request. Email providers also process those messages. Do not send card numbers, passwords, government IDs, or unnecessary sensitive information. A photo ID, page link, and short description are usually enough to start a review.

Retention and requests

There is no customer-upload archive or purchase database in this preview. Analytics and hosting records are governed by the relevant provider settings and policies; correspondence may be kept while needed to handle the request and related records. Contact us with questions or requests about information you provided. We may need enough information to confirm the request and your authority to make it.

Children and future changes

The planned submission and payment features are intended for adults 18 and older, not for children’s accounts. Before collecting real submissions or payments, we will update this notice to describe the actual providers, retention periods, privacy choices, and applicable rights. The planned service and these draft terms require review before paid launch.

Terms & Photo Permissions

  • Adult submitters. A submitter must be at least 18. For a photo of a minor, the submitter must be that child’s parent/legal guardian or have their permission for the public use described here.
  • Necessary rights. You must own the photograph’s copyright or have authorization from its owner covering these uses. You must also have any other necessary permission from people pictured or relevant rights holders. Possessing a photo—or appearing in it—does not necessarily make you its copyright owner. Family and memorial photos still require the necessary rights.
  • You keep ownership. The proposed license would be nonexclusive and limited to storing, reproducing, resizing, cropping, color-adjusting, arranging, and publicly displaying your photo within Picturepurdle, its saved editions, and its related viewing and sharing features. Service providers could process it only as needed to deliver those functions. Submission would not itself authorize unrelated AI-model training or standalone advertising/testimonials.
  • Public display. People could view, screenshot, copy, or share publicly displayed photos. Picturepurdle cannot guarantee prevention of copying or recall of copies made by others.
  • One-time placement. The proposed price is US$10 per approved photo, paid once with no annual renewal fee. Placement lasts while Picturepurdle operates, subject to legitimate removal requests, these rules, and applicable law. Continuous availability, an unchanging coordinate, or a particular overall portrait would not be guaranteed.
  • Removal. Contributors and people with legitimate ownership, privacy, or safety concerns could request removal. The removal process would need to address current and archived editions, backups, and applicable retention requirements. Outside copies cannot be recalled.
  • Giving plan. The proposed contribution is $2.50 from each eligible $10 purchase to World Central Kitchen, subject to an agreed program before launch. No partnership or endorsement is announced. Purchases are not presented as tax-deductible donations.

Planned confirmation before a real submission

“I am at least 18. I have the rights and permissions needed to submit these photos. I understand they will be publicly visible, and I agree to the Terms & Photo Permissions, Photo Rules, and Refund Policy.”

This is an example for review. Today’s local file preview does not obtain a license to publish your selected files. The real workflow must present the final terms, obtain affirmative acceptance, and keep an acceptance record before accepting public submissions.

Photo Rules

Yourself, someone you love, a person being remembered, and pets are welcome when you have the necessary permissions.

Pornography and sexually explicit material, sexual exploitation or sexualization of minors, graphic violence, threats, hateful harassment, unlawful content, impersonation, private information, and photos used without the required permissions would be prohibited.

Picturepurdle could refuse or remove submissions that breach the rules or reasonably raise privacy, ownership, or safety concerns. Automated screening would help flag problems; an AI flag alone would not establish wrongdoing or justify a forfeited payment. A review process would be available for mistakes. Repeated or deliberate violations could lead to submission restrictions.

Screening and these appeal processes are planned features. They are not connected to the local chooser in today’s preview.

Refund Policy

There are no charges or purchases in this preview. The following is the proposed approach for the paid service, subject to review and applicable law:

  • Before payment: photos rejected during initial screening would not be charged.
  • Service failure: if we charged for a photo but could not provide the purchased placement because of a service failure, the purchase would be refunded.
  • Confirmed deliberate violations: a photo knowingly submitted in violation of the rules, or to evade screening, would not be posted or would be removed. Payment could be nonrefundable only where permitted by law, following review.
  • Mistaken rejection: contributors could request review. An automated flag by itself would not result in a nonrefundable charge.
  • After publication: a change of mind alone would generally not qualify for a refund. Rights required by applicable law would remain available.
  • Billing problems: duplicate charges, alleged unauthorized payments, and missing placement would be reviewed. An amount eligible for refund would be returned to the original payment method where possible.

The final policy must specify request deadlines, processing times, cancellation rights, and any other applicable conditions before payment is enabled. Handling of the WCK portion of refunded purchases must also be settled in the giving agreement.

Contacting us does not limit your legal rights or a legitimate dispute through your payment provider.
